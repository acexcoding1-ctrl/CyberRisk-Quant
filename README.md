# 🛡️ Cyber Risk Quantification & Investment Optimization

> **Turning cybersecurity risk into numbers that businesses can understand.**

Cybersecurity teams often know that something is risky, but management also needs to know:

**“How much can this risk actually cost us?”**
**“Where should we invest our security budget?”**

That is what our platform is built to solve.

## 🚀 What Our Platform Does

Our dashboard combines **vulnerability data, threat intelligence, asset criticality and security controls** to continuously estimate an organization's cyber risk.

It helps organizations:

* 💰 Quantify cyber risk in financial terms
* 🔍 Identify the biggest risk drivers
* 🛡️ Measure the impact of security controls
* 📊 Simulate possible financial losses
* 💵 Compare security investments using **ROSI**
* 🎯 Optimize security spending within a given budget

## 💡 ROSI

One of our key features is **Return on Security Investment (ROSI)**.

Instead of simply saying:

> *“This security solution costs ₹10 lakh.”*

we try to answer:

> *“Will spending ₹10 lakh actually reduce more than ₹10 lakh worth of expected risk?”*

This connects **cybersecurity decisions with business value**.

## 🧠 How It Works

```text
Threat Intelligence + Enterprise Data
                ↓
          Risk Engine
                ↓
      Financial Risk Analysis
                ↓
     Controls + Monte Carlo
                ↓
       ROSI & Optimization
                ↓
          Dashboard
```

**Frontend:** React

**Cyber Data:** CVE, CVSS, EPSS, CISA KEV, MITRE ATT&CK

## 🎯 Our Goal

We don't want cybersecurity to be just:

**“Low / Medium / High.”**

We want to make it:

**“Here is the risk, here is what is causing it, here is how much it can cost, and here is where your next security investment can make a difference.”**

> **“Don’t just measure cyber risk. Measure what it means to the business.”**
